Penalty-free withdrawal: From Policy Year 5 to 14, the allowable amount is the lower of (a) X% of Policy Value less penalty-free withdrawals already made during that policy year, and (b) Policy Value less total premiums paid. Before the first actual withdrawal, X% follows the applicable policy year: 5% in PY5, increasing by 1% each year to 14% in PY14. Once the first actual withdrawal is made, that X% becomes fixed for the remainder of the surrender-charge period.
From Policy Year 15: the calculator no longer applies a surrender charge. Other prevailing withdrawal conditions and insurer processing requirements may still apply.
